# Mafer

Match the visual identity at https://mafer.ai/ (checked 2026-09-22):
Instrument Sans, a charcoal header with the white Mafer wordmark, white cards,
neutral gray backgrounds and borders, and restrained blue accents (#4397c6).
Use pale blue for selected rows, blue for measured signals, and charcoal for
reference spectra. Keep darker blue text for readable scores and controls.
Dark mode uses neutral grays and the same blue family.

Tokens live in `app/src/app/globals.css`. The wordmark in
`app/public/mafer-logo.svg` and locally hosted font files in `app/public/fonts/`
come from mafer.ai. Instrument Sans is distributed under the SIL Open Font
License included alongside the fonts. Keep the analyst layout compact, with
plots, components and candidates visible together on desktop.
